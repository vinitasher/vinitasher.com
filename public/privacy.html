Privacy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Browsing this site

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Your color-theme preference is stored in your browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the network information required to serve them.

The AI guide

When you ask a question, your message is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate an answer using Vinit’s public profile. The application does not save chat transcripts or send prior chat history with your next question. Messages remain visible only in the current page session. Please do not submit sensitive or confidential information.

Cloudflare processes requests and network information, including IP addresses. The application uses that network information for rate limiting and enables sampled operational logs and traces. Application error messages do not include your question. Cloudflare’s infrastructure may retain operational metadata according to its policies.

The guide is an AI assistant, not Vinit. It may make mistakes, and its responses are not professional commitments.

Contact

For privacy questions, email vinitasher@gmail.com. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for information about its processing.
